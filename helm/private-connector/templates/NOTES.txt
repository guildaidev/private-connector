One tunnel for connector {{ .Values.connector.id }} is configured.

Check: kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}
Logs: kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs deployment/{{ include "connector.name" . }} -c connector
Enrollment: kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs deployment/{{ include "connector.name" . }} -c enroll

After enrollment, delete the one-time enrollment Secret. Restarts reuse the PVC.
Do not scale this Deployment: each tunnel needs a separate release,
enrollment token, and identity volume. No Service or Ingress is required.

Uninstall ends the live session but does NOT revoke the tunnel or erase its identity.
Revoke the tunnel in Guild before deleting its retained PVC.
